# Tendly — Deviations (reality vs. stack)

> **Standing reconciliation rule:** when the founder — or anyone executing this stack — finds that reality has contradicted a stack document (the raise closed at a different valuation, a market figure proved wrong, a competitor moved, a cost came in higher), do **not** silently edit the stack to match. Record the finding here, dated, naming the contradicted claim/register ID. These entries are the raw material for **REVISE-STACK**, which edits the affected documents with stable IDs, re-runs only the affected gates, and regenerates the manifest + freeze hashes.
