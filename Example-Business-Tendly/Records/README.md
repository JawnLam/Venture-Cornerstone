# Records/ — history, not inputs

Everything in this folder explains **why** the stack is shaped the way it is: the founder interview (BI-1..BI-13 + BI-4b), the locked selection record (inclusions, exclusions, precedence, depth tier, audience bar), the source & dependency log, the stranger-test log with its verdict, the Gate-1 freeze hashes, and the cartridge manifest.

**None of it is needed to act on or execute the stack.** The consumer decides from `Stack/` alone; the founder executes from `Stack/` + `Workspace/`. Treat this folder as **immutable** — REVISE-STACK appends (a new stranger-test entry, a new freeze record), it never rewrites history.
