# Tendly — Founder Interview (BI-1..BI-13, plus BI-4b)

*Invented worked example. One question at a time; each answer is a citable line the selection record references. Every figure the founder brings is routed to `_source-log.md` / `_assumptions-register.md` — nothing is treated as fact because it was said in the interview.*

- **BI-1 · One-liner.** Tendly is the compliance operating system for independent childcare centers — it tracks staff certifications, licensed staff-to-child ratios, immunization and background-check records, and incident logs, so a center never fails a state licensing inspection. *(founder-supplied)*

- **BI-2 · Stage.** **Raising** — seed/angel, pre-Series-A. Not an idea: **12 paying centers are live today** at a `$43,200` ARR run-rate (BI-12). *(Most selection-shaping answer: real revenue but pre-scale — earns the money and market bands and the raise projections, but not a full operating plan or org chart.)*

- **BI-3 · Funding path.** **Equity — an angel round on a post-money SAFE.** Raising `$750,000`. **No debt** sought. *(Shapes deck, cap table, market sizing, financial rigor; excludes the SBA/bank loan plan.)*

- **BI-4 · Document audience.** **Angel investors** (primary), and the two co-founders must stay aligned. Not lenders, not a board yet. Angels here read a **deck**, a **one-pager**, and want the **model** behind the numbers — not a 30-page plan. *(Earns L0 alignment + the deck and one-pager projections; does not earn a formal SBA business plan.)*

- **BI-4b · Audience bar.** The angels Tendly is targeting write **`$25,000`–`$100,000` checks** on a **pre-seed thesis**, and several have told the founder they want to see **real retention** (durable paying centers, not just a logo count) and a **market they can believe** before committing; a lead-sized check would want the round substantially spoken for first. *(The `$25K`–`$100K` range and the stated must-sees are the founder's read of specific target angels, not signed commitments — stamped `[ASSUMPTION TND-A13]`.)* *(Selects nothing; it is the **bar Gate 2's ST-2 decision-plausibility is judged against** — "plausible for a `$25K`–`$100K` pre-seed angel who needs to see retention," not a generic reader. Without it, the stranger test grades against no one.)*

- **BI-5 · Business shape.** **B2B vertical SaaS**, runs continuously, sold **founder-led + inside sales** with a high-touch compliance onboarding. *(Shapes GTM; the continuous-run fact is real but the ops detail is thin at 12 centers — see BI-2.)*

- **BI-6 · Revenue model & pricing.** SaaS **subscription**; **`$299` per center per month** (founder-supplied published list price), annual plans offered. Pricing is known; blended ARPA is *modeled* at `$3,600`/center/year `[ASSUMPTION TND-A1]` (list × 12, rounded for occasional multi-room add-ons).

- **BI-7 · Market.** Founder *believes* there are roughly **130,000 licensed center-based childcare facilities in the US** but has **no sourced figure yet** — this is the single biggest research gap, and every TAM/SAM/SOM number in this stack is therefore an `[ASSUMPTION]` to be replaced before investor use. Competitors: (a) childcare-management suites that do billing/attendance/parent-comms and treat compliance as a checkbox (a Brightwheel-class category); (b) spreadsheets, paper binders, and "do nothing" — the real status quo; (c) horizontal HR/certification-tracking tools not built for childcare licensing rules. *(Earns the market brief; the missing source is why the data discipline exists.)*

- **BI-8 · Team.** **Two co-founders** — CEO (former childcare-center director, the domain and sales voice) and CTO (full-stack engineer). Post-raise hiring: **1 full-stack engineer + 1 customer-success lead**. *(>1 founder → cap table + founder agreement + entity earned; the hiring plan folds into use-of-funds, not a standalone org chart.)*

- **BI-9 · Regulatory & IP exposure.** Tendly is **not the regulated party** — the centers are — but it **stores children's and staff PII**: background-check status, immunization records, incident reports. That creates a real **data-privacy and terms-of-service exposure** requiring counsel. **Not IP-heavy; no patents.** *(Earns a data-handling/ToS flag under L6; excludes a patent/IP-assignment document.)*

- **BI-10 · Timeline.** Close the round **Q4 2026**. The `$750,000` funds **~18 months** of runway to **~200 centers (`$720,000` ARR)**, the threshold for a Series A conversation around **H2 2028**.

- **BI-11 · Money crossing a boundary.** **Investor capital via a SAFE — a security** — and ongoing customer subscription revenue. *(The SAFE and the cap table are securities-law surface → `requires-signoff-by` a securities attorney + CPA; this is the authority boundary, Decision 4.)*

- **BI-12 · Founder-supplied assets.** Current **12 paying centers / `$43,200` ARR** (internal billing export); **11 of 12 pilots retained ≥6 months** (directional retention, n=12); published list price **`$299`/mo**. **No third-party market research** — the founder has run zero sourced TAM work. *(All of this enters the source log as founder-supplied; the absent market research is logged as the open dependency.)*

- **BI-13 · Current cash on hand.** **`$120,000` in the bank today** (internal bank/accounting balance) against a **current net burn of ~`$18,000`/mo** — two founders, pre-hire: gross burn ~`$21,600`/mo less current subscription revenue ~`$3,600`/mo (`$43,200` ARR ÷ 12). That is **~6.7 months of current runway** (`120,000 ÷ 18,000`), which is the clock dating the Q4-2026 close (BI-10). *(Founder-supplied — enters `_assumptions-register.md` as `TND-A11` cash / `TND-A12` current net burn and `_source-log.md` `TND-S9`. It is the **mandatory runway input**: without it the model would silently assume `$0` starting cash. Post-raise burn rises to `$42,000`/mo, `TND-A6` — a different number for a different phase.)*

## Reflected picture (confirmed before selection)

A revenue-stage but pre-scale vertical-SaaS business raising a small angel round on a SAFE, with two founders, real-but-thin traction, sticky compliance-critical software, PII exposure, `$120,000` cash on ~`$18K`/mo current burn (~6.7 months, BI-13), an angel audience that writes `$25K`–`$100K` pre-seed checks and wants to see retention (BI-4b), and — critically — **strong founder-supplied operating numbers but no sourced market numbers at all**. It earns the unconditional L1 model and V verification; a standalone L0 vision brief (angels must be convinced); the L2 market brief (equity raise + a market that must be argued); the L3 financial summary with unit economics (money is crossing a boundary); the L4 pitch deck and one-pager projections (angel audience). It flags — but does not bless — the L6 cap table, entity, and founder agreement (securities + two founders + PII). It does **not** earn: an SBA/bank loan plan (no debt), a formal SBA business plan (angels decide from deck + model), a standalone operating/org plan (pre-scale, team of two), or an IP/patent document (not IP-heavy).
