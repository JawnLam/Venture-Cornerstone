# Tendly — Lean Canvas (L1 · Model, unconditional)

> The one unconditional model document (canon rule 1). Nine Lean Canvas blocks (Maurya), all filled. Owns the **model**; on any figure it cites the assumptions register (`TND-A<n>`) and defers to `04-financial-summary.md`.

## 1. Problem
- Independent childcare centers must continuously prove staff-to-child ratios, current staff certifications, and complete immunization/background/incident records, or risk citations, fines, or license suspension.
- Today this runs on paper binders, wall calendars, and the director's memory — hours a week of pure-downside clerical work.
- Existing childcare software (billing/attendance/parent-comms) treats compliance as a checkbox, not the product.
- **Existing alternatives:** spreadsheets + paper binders (`TND-S7`); the compliance module bolted onto a childcare-management suite (`TND-S6`).

## 2. Customer Segments
- **Primary:** independent, owner-operated childcare centers, 1–3 locations, ~40–120 licensed capacity, in licensing-heavy states. Buyer = owner or director.
- **Early adopters:** directors who have recently taken a citation or a corrective-action plan, or are prepping for a renewal inspection. (Full ICP in `03-market-brief.md`.)

## 3. Unique Value Proposition
- **"Never fail an inspection you could have seen coming."** Tendly turns a center's compliance from a binder-and-memory scramble into a live dashboard that flags every expiring certification and ratio risk before an inspector does.
- **High-level concept:** the compliance operating system for childcare — purpose-built for licensing rules, not a billing app with a compliance tab.

## 4. Solution
- Certification-expiry tracking with graduated lead-time alerts.
- Live staff-to-child ratio monitoring against the classroom's licensed limits.
- A records vault for immunization, background-check, and incident data.
- One-click, inspection-ready report generation.

## 5. Channels
- Founder-led outbound to directors (the CEO is a former director) + inside sales (`TND-S`/GTM in `03-market-brief.md`).
- Word of mouth within local director networks and licensing-prep communities; referral loops between neighboring centers.

## 6. Revenue Streams
- SaaS subscription: **`$299` per center per month** (`TND-S3`), annual plans offered.
- Modeled blended ARPA **`$3,600`/center/yr** (`TND-A1`).
- Current: 12 centers → **`$43,200` ARR** (`TND-S1`). Plan: `$180K` / `$720K` / `$2.16M` ARR end-Y1/Y2/Y3 (`TND-A5`, `TND-A9`).

## 7. Cost Structure
- Team (2 founders → +1 engineer +1 customer-success post-raise); cloud hosting + support (gross margin **80%**, `TND-A2`); customer acquisition (blended CAC **`$2,400`**, `TND-A3`).
- Average net burn **post-raise `$42,000`/mo** (`TND-A6`; current pre-raise ~`$18,000`/mo, `TND-A12`).

## 8. Key Metrics
- Paying centers; net ARR; annual logo churn (target 18%, `TND-A4` — the metric the whole model hinges on); CAC payback (**10 months**, `TND-A3`); LTV:CAC (**6.7:1**, flagged optimistic).
- Activation proxy: a center that has produced its first inspection-ready report from Tendly.

## 9. Unfair Advantage
- **Switching resistance by construction:** once a center's license-critical records live in Tendly, migrating out is migrating the thing its license depends on.
- **Domain founder:** the CEO is a former center director — earns trust and reads licensing rules the incumbents treat generically.
- **Compliance depth as the wedge:** the billing-first incumbents cannot easily follow without rebuilding around per-state licensing logic, which is not their core.
