# Tendly — Vision & Problem Brief (L0 · Why)

> Answers one question: **why does this business deserve to exist?** Owns the problem narrative; owns no figures. Any number here cites its `TND-` anchor and defers to `04-financial-summary.md` / `03-market-brief.md`.

## The problem (TND-C1)

An independent childcare center lives and dies by its state license. Keeping it means proving, continuously, that every classroom is within its staff-to-child ratio, that every staff member's CPR/first-aid/background-check/training certifications are current, that immunization and incident records are complete, and that all of it can be produced on demand when a licensing inspector walks in — often unannounced. Today the typical owner-operated center runs this on a paper binder, a wall calendar, and the director's memory. A single expired certification or a ratio lapse discovered on inspection can mean a citation, a fine, a corrective-action plan, or — for a repeat finding — suspension. The director spends hours a week on compliance clerical work that is pure downside risk: it never grows the business, and getting it wrong can end it.

## Why now (TND-C2)

Two shifts make this the moment. First, state licensing regimes have grown more documentation-heavy and more inclined toward unannounced, records-first inspections, raising the cost of the paper-binder status quo. Second, the incumbent childcare software the founder sees in the field is built for **billing, attendance, and parent communication** — compliance is a shallow afterthought, not the product. That leaves the highest-stakes, most anxiety-inducing part of running a center essentially un-served by purpose-built software.

## The business (TND-C3)

**Tendly** is the compliance operating system for independent childcare centers: certification-expiry tracking with lead-time alerts, live ratio monitoring, a records vault for immunization/background-check/incident data, and one-click generation of inspection-ready reports. It is sold to the owner or director at **`$299` per center per month** (`TND-S3`), and it is **sticky by construction** — once a center's compliance records live in Tendly, moving them out is moving the thing the license depends on, which is why the model assumes low logo churn (`TND-A4`).

## Who this is for, and the ask

The customer is the **independent, owner-operated center** (1–3 locations) — described precisely in `03-market-brief.md`. Tendly has **12 paying centers today** (`$43,200` ARR, `TND-S1`) and is raising a **`$750,000`** angel round (`TND-A10`) to reach ~200 centers over ~18 months. The full case — market, unit economics, and the ask — is carried by the documents this brief precedes; this brief exists only to establish that the problem is real, expensive, unavoidable, and currently un-served. That is the "why." The "whether the numbers work" is `04-financial-summary.md`.
