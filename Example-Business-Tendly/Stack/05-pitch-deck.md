## Projection discipline (read before editing)

- **Every figure above is a citation, not a source.** To change a number, change it in the source document; the deck follows. This is the whole reason the deck is a projection and not a fourth copy of the numbers.
- **The market-size slide (`TND-D5`) must visibly carry its `[ASSUMPTION]` stamp.** A polished deck that presents an unsourced TAM as fact is the classic stack-poisoning failure — it costs credibility at the exact moment the business is asking for money. The honest version wins the room it needs to win.
- **No slide restates the cap table or SAFE terms as settled** — `TND-D10` states the *ask*; the instrument is counsel's (`04-financial-summary` §5).
