# Tendly — One-Pager (L4 · projection)

> Fast top-of-funnel read for an angel (BI-4). A **projection** — cites, never restates; owns no figure. Every number carries its `TND-` anchor and equals the source.

**Tendly — the compliance operating system for independent childcare centers.**

**Problem.** Independent centers run license-critical compliance — staff-to-child ratios, certification expiry, immunization/background/incident records — on paper binders and memory. One gap found on an unannounced, records-first inspection means citations, fines, or license suspension. Incumbent childcare software (billing/attendance/parent-comms) treats compliance as a checkbox. *(`01-vision-brief`)*

**Product.** A live dashboard: certification-expiry alerts, ratio monitoring, a records vault, and one-click inspection-ready reports. Sticky by construction — a center's license depends on the records now living in Tendly. *(`02-lean-canvas` §4/§9)*

**Model.** `$299`/center/month (`TND-S3`); ARPA `$3,600`/yr (`TND-A1`); gross margin 80% (`TND-A2`); CAC `$2,400` → **10-month payback** (`TND-A3`); LTV:CAC 6.7:1 *(assumption-driven, churn-sensitive)*. *(`04-financial-summary` §1)*

**Market.** TAM ~`$468M` / SAM ~`$100M` — **`[ASSUMPTION / UNVERIFIED]`**, pending a sourced figure (`TND-A7/A8`, `03-market-brief`). Stated as an assumption on purpose.

**Traction.** **12 paying centers, `$43,200` ARR** today (`TND-S1`); 11 of 12 pilots retained ≥6 months (`TND-S2`).

**Plan.** `$180K` / `$720K` / **`$2.16M`** ARR end-Y1/Y2/Y3 (50 / 200 / 600 centers, `TND-A5`).

**Team.** 2 co-founders — CEO (former childcare director), CTO (full-stack); +1 engineer, +1 customer-success post-raise. *(BI-8)*

**The ask.** **`$750,000` on a post-money SAFE, `$6.0M` cap** (~12.5%, `TND-A10`) → ~18-month runway to ~200 centers (`$720,000` ARR). *SAFE + cap table `requires-signoff-by` securities counsel + CPA (`04-financial-summary` §5).*
